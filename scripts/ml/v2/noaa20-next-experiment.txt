Required new experiment
Neither the released v1 nor the frozen v2 S-NPP model is validated for NOAA-20.
Do not replace a live sensor URL and reuse S-NPP probabilities or calibration.
NOAA-20 has different sampling times and detection outcomes. It needs a distinct
model identity, matching training labels, independent evaluation and provenance.

A tractable first experiment can reuse the WSTS weather/terrain TIFFs while
rebuilding the prior, recent and target fire masks from NOAA-20 points for every
fixed event window. Preserve UTC acquisition dates, confidence filtering and a
documented point-to-grid footprint; verify alignment and rasterization against
the intended live NOAA-20 feed. Include all outcomes, including target-zero
events. Never choose or replace events after seeing their target labels.
Dates before NOAA-20 coverage starts are unavailable, not empty fire masks.

Reusing WSTS also reuses its S-NPP-based event selection. This limits the claim
to those sampled event contexts and does not establish performance for all
NOAA-20 fires or nationwide ignition prediction. An expanded study should sample
events from the continuing sensor itself. Check historical outage/observation
availability where possible; a missing hotspot still does not prove clear-sky
coverage or an unburned cell. Country archives may omit cross-border inputs;
either retrieve matching neighboring coverage or declare/handle that edge.

Freeze a new metadata-only allocation before inspecting new NOAA-20 outcomes:
separate fitting, calibration and model/threshold selection from an untouched
test, retaining geographic/incident separation. If using fresh 2021 events,
exclude every already inspected v1/v2 test set and nearby incident complexes;
choose no replacement IDs in this note. A later held-out year is another option
if matching historical covariates can be obtained without future leakage.
Report same-cell, same-weight comparisons with persistence and the prior model
where their targets are meaningfully comparable, plus uncertainty and source
availability subgroups. An S-NPP model evaluated against NOAA-20 outcomes is a
cross-sensor baseline, not a previously validated comparator.

Validate the actual live NOAA-20 source and transformations separately. Reusing
WSTS weather leaves the existing live weather/terrain transfer differences in
place; historical test skill alone does not validate current probabilities.
Keep S-NPP cessation safeguards until a continuing-sensor model and its live
pipeline pass this separate protocol. No bulk download, new target masks,
additional training or test selection was performed for this note.
